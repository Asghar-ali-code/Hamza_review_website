Privacy

By submitting your review, you agree that your feedback and chosen display name may be published publicly. Please do not include private information about yourself or others.

This page stores the display name you choose, your star rating, your message, and the time you submit it. Hamza uses that to decide what appears here. It does not ask for your email, phone number, or photos, and it does not publish the flat's address. New reviews stay unpublished until they are approved.

Removing a review

To take a review down, ask Hamza in person. He can remove it before or after it is published.
